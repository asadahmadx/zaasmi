## What it does

Zaasmi is a desktop AI copilot that listens to your live calls and shows you what to say — in about three seconds, grounded in your own sales material. It runs in a private overlay that's hidden from screen sharing, so no one on the call ever knows it's there. No bot joins the meeting and nothing is recorded.

## Features

- ⚡ **Real-time answers in ~3 seconds** — suggestions appear almost as fast as the question lands.
- 📚 **Grounded in your own skills files** — upload your playbook, pricing, and objection handling (Markdown, PDF, or Word) and get answers that use *your* actual numbers.
- 🏷️ **SAY NOW / LATER tags** — every suggestion is labeled so you know what to say immediately versus what to keep in your back pocket.
- 🕵️ **Private overlay, hidden from screen share** — the panel is excluded from screen capture at the OS level, so it never shows up in shared screens or recordings.
- 🚫 **No bot joins the call** — Zaasmi runs on your machine, not as a meeting participant. Guests see a normal call.
- 🔒 **No transcripts stored** — your audio is transcribed to text locally and discarded; nothing is kept on our servers.
- 🎥 **Works with Zoom, Google Meet & Microsoft Teams** — and anything else that plays audio through your computer.

## FAQ

**Does the copilot show up when I share my screen?**
No. With private screen-share mode on, the panel is excluded from screen capture at the OS level, so it isn't visible in shared screens or recordings.

**Do I need my own API key?**
No. Zaasmi is fully managed — the AI is included in your plan. There's nothing to configure.

**What happens to my call audio?**
It never leaves your device. Zaasmi transcribes your speech to text locally and discards it — nothing is stored on our servers.

**Which call platforms does it work with?**
Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams — and anything else that plays audio through your computer.

**How fast do suggestions appear?**
About three seconds from when the question finishes.

**Can Zaasmi use my own playbook and pricing?**
Yes. Upload skill files in Markdown, PDF, or Word, and suggestions are grounded in your actual services and numbers.
