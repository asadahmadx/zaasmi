# Changelog

User-facing changes to the Zaasmi desktop app. Download the latest build at
[zaasmi.com/downloads](https://zaasmi.com/downloads).

## v0.1.7 — 2026-11-17

- **Redesigned subscription screen** — clearer plan, billing cycle, renewal date, payment method, and invoices, all in one place.
- **Pause instead of cancel** — heading out for a while? Pause your plan with no charge while it's paused, and pick up right where you left off. Your skills and setup are kept so nothing is lost.
- **Smoother cancellation** — a simpler, clearer flow if you do decide to leave, with your access continuing until the end of your billing period.
- **Come-back offers** — a friendly reminder and a discount if you'd like to return later.
- Reliability and performance improvements.

## v0.1.6 — 2026-11-13

- Performance and stability improvements across the app.
- Updated the underlying app runtime for better reliability and security.
- Various small fixes to billing and account screens.
